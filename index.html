About

I'm Shane — a K-pop producer and sound designer. Between sessions I build the tools I want to use: plugins that do one thing without a manual. The hardware models are calibrated against the real units (a real Minimoog, a real plate reverb, measured band by band), every plugin ships with a built-in 3-day trial, and there's a ghost on every panel whose mood follows what the plugin is doing to your audio.

It's just me — I answer every message personally. Press & reviewer NFR licences and distribution enquiries welcome.
