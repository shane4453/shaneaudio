Envelope shaping split across three bands, so the low end and the top can be treated separately from one trigger.

Set the trigger, choose which band drives it, and each band follows with its own response — useful for ducking a bass under a kick without pulling the whole mix down with it.

CONTROLS
· TRIGGER      what drives the shaping
· FILTER MODE · FILTER LOW · FILTER HIGH   which band listens
· OUTPUT
· BYPASS
