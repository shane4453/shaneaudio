A flanger with a real through-zero mode — the two paths cross and the notch

walks straight through DC, the way it only ever happened with two tape

machines.


CONTROLS

· MODE classic, or through-zero

· MANUAL 0.1 to 10 ms — park the notch by hand

· RATE down to a crawl, up to 5 Hz, or locked to your project tempo

· DEPTH 0 to 100%

· FEEDBACK 0 to 10, positive or negative — the sign is its own control

· MIX 0 to 100%

· STEREO spread the two sides apart

· FB HIGHPASS 20 Hz to 1 kHz, so the feedback doesn't pile up in the low end

· Input and output meters, and a true bypass


The rate knob is deliberately slow at the bottom — one sweep can take well

over ten seconds.


Watch the ghost next to the logo. There are two of them, one per tap, and

they drift apart and back together as the delay between them opens and

closes. That is the flanger, drawn.

INSTALL — WINDOWS

1. Unzip.

2. Copy "Shane Flanger.vst3" to:

C:\\Program Files\\Common Files\\VST3

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.


INSTALL — macOS

1. Open the .pkg installer and follow the prompts.

2. Pick the formats you want (VST3 / AU / AAX).

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.
