A two-channel analog phaser. Four, eight, or twelve stages, with each side

running its own speed, filter and pan — or linked together when you want

them locked.

CONTROLS

· STAGES 4, 8 or 12

· SPEED how fast it sweeps

· FILTER where the notches sit

· PAN move each channel across the field

· DRY / WET blend it back in

· FEEDBACK with a randomize button

· OSCILLATORS 90° or 180° between the two sides

· SYNC lock the sweep to your project

· WAVE SELECT pick the sweep shape

· LINK lock both channels together, control by control

· Input and output level, with VU metering on both

Watch the ghost at the bottom — it sweeps across the panel with a trail

behind it, in time with the phase.

INSTALL — WINDOWS

1. Unzip.

2. Copy "Shane Phaser.vst3" to:

C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.

INSTALL — macOS

1. Open the .pkg installer and follow the prompts.

2. Pick the formats you want (VST3 / AU / AAX).

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.
