A 1073-style channel strip: a preamp that colours as you push it, and the four-band EQ that sits behind it.

The drive is modelled on transformer saturation, so the harmonics fall off with frequency the way iron does — thick low end, clean top.

CONTROLS

· GAIN mic 20–80 dB in 5 dB steps, or line

· HIGH SHELF 12 kHz

· MID peak — 0.36 / 0.7 / 1.6 / 3.2 / 4.8 / 7.2 kHz

· LOW SHELF — 35 / 60 / 110 / 220 Hz

· HIGH-PASS — 50 / 80 / 160 / 300 Hz

· EQL in/out and PHASE
