A four-track cassette machine. Not one pass to tape — the 424 sound comes

from bouncing tracks onto each other, and every bounce costs you something.


GENERATIONS

Run it one to four passes deep. Each generation shaves the top, piles on

noise, and adds another layer of wobble. This is the knob the whole

plugin is built around.


THE TAPE

· BIAS / TAPE TYPE low is gritty, high is smooth but erases the top

· TAPE SPEED 7½ · 3¾ · 1⅞ ips

· WOW · FLUTTER slow drift and fast quiver

· HISS louder than reel-to-reel, because the tracks are narrow

· MODULATION NOISE the hiss swells with the signal, the way cassette does

· AZIMUTH head misalignment — shaves the top, skews L/R phase

· dbx NR real 2:1 companding, folded on record and unfolded on

play, so only what the tape added gets pushed down

· PRINT-THROUGH bleed into the neighbouring wind, heard one turn later

as a dull echo — dull because long wavelengths bleed most

· PITCH CONTROL varispeed, by hand


THE MIXER

· TRIM · INPUT (mic / line / tape)

· EQ HIGH · EQ LOW

· MIXER GRIT the console's own noise and nonlinearity

· PAN · BUS LEVEL · MASTER

· RECORD FUNCTION direct / record / safe


Watch the ghost in the tape window — it drifts side to side with the wow.
