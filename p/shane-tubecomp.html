A vari-mu tube compressor. Slow and round when you want it to breathe,

fast and grabby when you don't — with a de-bumpify control that keeps the

low end from pumping the whole mix around.

WHAT'S IN IT

· INPUT drive into the tube stage

· THRESHOLD how hard it grabs

· ATTACK slow to fast

· RELEASE how it lets go

· DE-BUMPIFY tames low-end pumping

· FILTER keeps lows out of the detector

· MAKE UP bring the level back

· DRY / WET parallel compression in one knob

· AUTO GAIN

· Backlit gain-reduction VU meter
