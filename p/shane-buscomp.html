The glue compressor for your mix bus — the one you leave on at 2 dB and forget about.

The colour changes with how hard it works. Most emulations add a fixed amount of harmonic content no matter what you do; on the real thing the even harmonics come up as the compression deepens, so it thickens as it grabs. That's modelled here, and it's why it sounds different at 6 dB than at 1 dB.

The fade control is for the mix bus specifically: it eases the compressor in and out over a set time instead of switching, so you can A/B without the jump.
