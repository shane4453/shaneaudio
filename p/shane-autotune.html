Real-time tuning with a retune speed that goes from transparent to fully snapped, and a harmony macro that follows the corrected note.

Note selection and the correction reference are separate, so you can keep a note out of the scale without detuning the ones you keep.

CONTROLS

· RETUNE SPEED transparent to hard

· HUMANIZE holds sustained notes back from the grid

· FLEX-TUNE leaves off-scale notes alone

· NATURAL VIBRATO

· HARMONY macro — a third and a fifth that track the corrected pitch

· KEY and SCALE, or pick notes on the keyboard

· DETUNE reference · TRACKING · MIX

· Input voice type, HOLD, latch or momentary keyboard

REGISTRATION

· The plug-in runs unrestricted for a 3-day trial from first launch.

· Your licence key is on your Gumroad receipt and download page.

· Paste it into the plug-in once — it needs internet that one time, then works offline.

INSTALL — WINDOWS

1. Unzip.

2. Copy the .vst3 to C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.

INSTALL — macOS

1. Open the .pkg installer and follow the prompts.

2. Pick the formats you want (VST3 / AU / AAX).

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.

Signed and notarized by Apple — no Terminal, no security workaround.

The installer asks for your admin password because the plug-ins go to /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins (available to every user on the Mac).
