A monophonic bass synth built around a ladder filter, tuned against measurements of the real circuit rather than a textbook formula.

The filter envelope discharges like an RC network, which is what gives the low end its snap instead of a flat sweep.

CONTROLS

· Two oscillators with wave and octave, DETUNE, filter envelope amount

· MIXER — OSC 1 · OSC 2 · SUB · NOISE

· FILTER — CUTOFF · RES · DRIVE · KB TRK

· Filter and amp envelopes (ATK · DEC · SUS · REL)

· LFO — RATE · SHAPE · to PITCH and FILTER

· GLIDE · VOLUME · MONO
