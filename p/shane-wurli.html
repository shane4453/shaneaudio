A sampled Wurlitzer 200A with the reed bark intact — the growl you get when you dig in, not just a louder version of the soft notes.

Every note was retuned before it went in. The source recordings were 31 cents flat across the board, several notes were labelled an octave off, and the top octave was unusable. All of that is fixed: the instrument now averages 0.4 cents of error across the keyboard.

The 200A's own effects are here too — the tremolo that made the instrument famous, plus the phaser, chorus and reverb people always ran it through.

CONTROLS
· VOLUME · DRIVE       preamp level and how hard you push the amp
· BASS / TREBLE        the 200A's own tone stack
· TONE                 Normal · Medium · Hard — how bright the reeds sit
· TREMOLO              speed and intensity, the classic 200A wobble
· PHASER               rate and depth
· CHORUS               rate and depth
· REVERB               mix
· OUTPUT               final level

Watch the ghost on the panel — it sways with the tremolo.
