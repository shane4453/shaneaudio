A programmable polyphonic synthesizer with the full front panel out in the

open — every slider where you can reach it, and the patch memory underneath.

SYNTH

· LFO rate and delay time

· DCO 16' / 8' / 4' range, LFO, PWM, sub and noise, square or saw

· HPF high pass before the filter

· VCF frequency, resonance, envelope amount, LFO, key follow

· VCA envelope or gate

· ENV attack, decay, sustain, release

· CHORUS off / I / II

PATCH MEMORY

Eight banks of eight patches, with manual mode, write, save, verify and load.

Factory presets included.

INSTALL — WINDOWS

1. Unzip.

2. Copy "Shane 106.vst3" to:

C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.

INSTALL — macOS

1. Open the .pkg installer and follow the prompts.

2. Pick the formats you want (VST3 / AU / AAX).

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.
