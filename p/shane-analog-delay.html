CONTROLS

· DELAY TIME 1ms to 2000ms, free or synced to host tempo

· SYNC / TAP note divisions down to triplets, or tap it in

· MODES rhythm · ping pong · dual · L-R link

· FEEDBACK how long it hangs on

· MODULATION depth, rate, and shape — sine, triangle, square

· FILTERS hipass and lowpass on the repeats, linkable

· SPREAD widen the ping pong across the field

· LPF OFFSET darken one side of the stereo image

· SATURATION tape grit on the tail

· ANALOG dark to bright character

· WIDTH mono to wide

· DRY / WET and output level

Watch the ghost on the panel — it leaves an echo trail behind it.
