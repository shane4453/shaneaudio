A tube opto compressor. Slow, round, and forgiving — the kind you leave on

the whole mix and forget about.


CONTROLS

· THRESHOLD -40 to +30 dB

· RATIO 1.5:1 up to 10:1

· ATTACK 0.5 ms to 300 ms

· RELEASE 50 ms to 10 seconds

· OUTPUT ±30 dB of make-up

· PARALLEL 0 to 100% — blend the dry back in without a second channel

· SELECT fixed, manual, or fixed/manual for attack and release

· LOW CUT off · 80 Hz · 220 Hz on the detector

· GENERATION vintage or modern

· BUS SELECT internal or external sidechain

· METER switch the VU between input, compressor and output


Watch the ghost on the right — it gets squashed flat right along with

everything else. The more gain reduction, the flatter it goes.

INSTALL — WINDOWS

1. Unzip.

2. Copy "Shane 1B.vst3" to:

C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.


INSTALL — macOS

1. Open the .pkg installer and follow the prompts.

2. Pick the formats you want (VST3 / AU / AAX).

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.
