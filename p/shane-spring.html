A spring tank — one, two or three springs, with the boing and the shake you only get from actual springs.

Springs disperse: high frequencies travel faster than low ones, so a transient arrives as a chirp rather than a copy. That dispersion is where the sound comes from, and it's modelled with real allpass chains rather than faked with a filter.

CONTROLS
· SPRINGS      One · Two · Three
· MIX · PRE-DELAY · TENSION
· BOING        the characteristic chirp
· SHAKE        kick the tank
· DAMPING      how fast it dies
· BASS · TREBLE · WIDTH · OUTPUT
