A sampled vintage polysynth with a filter section you can reshape — seven filter types including a phaser and a formant mode.

Presets are tuned to whole octaves against the source recordings rather than to the nearest cent, so patches stay in tune with each other when you layer them.

CONTROLS
· OSC2         second layer
· FILTER TYPE  Off · 2 Pole · 4 Pole · C-Type · Phaser · Form · Classic
· FILTER MODE  LPF · BPF · HPF · Notch
· FILTER SLOPE
· LFO WAVE · LFO SYNC
· PLAY MODE
· CHORUS
