CONTROLS

· ATTACK .1 to 10

· RELEASE 10 to 1k

· RATIO 1:1 up to 20:1

· MAKEUP bring the level back, ±24 dB

· SC HPF keep the lows out of the detector, 10 Hz to 1k

· LINKED tie both channels together, or let them work apart
