Two knobs and a curve. Drag the frequency, decide how hard to damp it, and

get out. It tells you the note you're sitting on, not just the number.

CONTROLS

· AMOUNT how hard it damps

· PINCH how narrow it gets

· Drag anywhere on the curve to set the frequency

The readout shows Hz and the musical note with cents — 615 Hz D#5-22,

80 Hz D#2+38. Handy when the thing you're fighting is a note, not a number.

INSTALL — WINDOWS

1. Unzip.

2. Copy "Shane Damper.vst3" to:

C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.

INSTALL — macOS

1. Open the .pkg installer and follow the prompts.

2. Pick the formats you want (VST3 / AU / AAX).

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.
