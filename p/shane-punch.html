Attack and sustain on separate controls, each with its own frequency range, so you can add snap to a snare without touching the room behind it.

The envelope follower is symmetric, which matters more than it sounds: an asymmetric follower leaves residual gain on steady material, so a sustained note slowly drifts in level. This one measures 0.000 dB of change on steady signal regardless of how loud it is — it only moves when something actually transient happens.

CONTROLS
· TRANSIENT · SUSTAIN     the two halves, cut or boost
· TIME                    how long the shaper considers a transient
· TRANSIENT FOCUS LOW/HIGH   band the attack works on
· SUSTAIN FOCUS LOW/HIGH     band the sustain works on
