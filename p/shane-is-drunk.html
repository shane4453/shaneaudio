Two knobs. One of them is how drunk it is. A slow LFO wanders left and right out of phase, and on top of that each channel takes its own random walk — so the two sides never agree on where they are. Push it past halfway and it starts to hiccup. Under 30% it is a chorus. Past 70% it is a vibrato that cannot walk straight. In between is where it is most fun.
