Pitch and formant on separate controls — move one without dragging the other along.

The shifting is PSOLA, which keeps the voice sounding like a voice instead of a phase vocoder's version of one. Formant is genuinely independent, so you can drop a vocal an octave and keep the throat size, or leave the pitch alone and change the size of the singer.

Three modes: transpose by interval, quantize to a scale, or lock to a single note for the robot sound.

CONTROLS
· PITCH · FORMANT      independent
· LINK                 tie them together when you want the natural version
· MODE                 Transpose · Quantize · Robot
· ROBOT NOTE           the note Robot mode locks to
· POLY                 for material with more than one voice at a time
· DRIVE · MIX · OUTPUT · BYPASS
