Odd and even harmonics on separate knobs, so you can build the distortion you want rather than picking a preset flavour.

Even harmonics thicken and warm. Odd harmonics bite and cut. Most distortion gives you a fixed blend of the two and calls it a mode — here they're two controls, and the tone knob sits after them so you can shape what came out.

Runs at 4x oversampling so the harmonics you added stay where you put them instead of folding back down the spectrum.

CONTROLS
· DRIVE           input into the stage
· ODD HARMONICS   bite and cut
· EVEN HARMONICS  thickness and warmth
· MODE            Symmetric · Tube Screamer · Asymmetric
· TONE            shapes what comes out
· ANALOG          circuit character
· MIX             parallel blend
· OVERSAMPLE 4x   keeps the added harmonics from folding back
· OUTPUT
