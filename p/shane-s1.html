Plug a guitar in and get a synth out — tracking your playing, not triggering samples.

Everything depends on the front end. The pitch detector runs YIN at 8 kHz with parabolic interpolation, because a synth that mistracks is useless no matter how good the oscillators are. There's latency in any honest pitch tracker; this one hides it rather than pretending it isn't there.

CONTROLS
· EFFECT   which synth voice you're driving
· CTRL 1 · CTRL 2   the two hands-on controls for the current voice
· PRESET   step through the voices
· DRY      your guitar, blended back in
· BYPASS
