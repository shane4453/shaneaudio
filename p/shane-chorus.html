A stompbox chorus with a vibrato mode on its own footswitch. Two pedals in

one box — thick stereo chorus, or pitch-only vibrato with nothing dry

underneath.

CONTROLS

· CHORUS INTENSITY how thick it gets

· VIBRATO DEPTH how far it swings

· VIBRATO RATE how fast it swings

· LEVEL CONTROL output level, with a peak LED

· DUAL / CLASSIC two voicings

· MONO / STEREO output mode

· EFFECT footswitch true bypass

· CHORUS footswitch switch between chorus and vibrato

PRESETS

Default · CE-1 Classic · Deep Wash · Vibrato 6ms · Dual Ensemble · Subtle Wobble

Watch the ghost on the pedal — it wobbles side to side with the modulation.

INSTALL — WINDOWS

1. Unzip.

2. Copy "Shane Chorus.vst3" to:

C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.

INSTALL — macOS

1. Open the .pkg installer and follow the prompts.

2. Pick the formats you want (VST3 / AU / AAX).

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.

Signed and notarized by Apple — no Terminal, no security workaround.
