A four-band EQ with shelves on the outside, two parametric mids in the

BANDS

· LF low shelf — gain and frequency

· LMF parametric — gain, frequency and Q

· HMF parametric — gain, frequency and Q

· HF high shelf — gain and frequency

ALSO

· FOCUS tightens either mid band when you need to get surgical

· HPF high pass, off up to 50 Hz

· TRIM ±12 dB output

· IN bypass, so you can check your work

· Live curve display over the audio
