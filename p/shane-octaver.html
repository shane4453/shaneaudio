An octave pedal with five voices on faders — two octaves down, two up, and

your dry signal — so you build the stack instead of picking a preset shape.


VOICE MIX

· DRY OUTPUT

· −2 · −1 OCTAVE the sub. Rolled toward a triangle rather than a square,

so it sits under the note instead of buzzing against it

· +1 · +2 OCTAVE organ, twelve-string, ring


EFFECTS

· ATTACK swell the octaves in behind the pick

· LP FILTER take the fizz off the upper voices

· DETUNE drift the voices apart

· DRY FX run the effects on the dry signal too

· Q resonance on the filter


PRESETS

Eight banks on the panel — Sub Bass, Deep Sub, Octave Up, Organ,

12-String, Synth Swell, Ring Up, Full Stack. Hold a bank to save.


Watch the ghost above DRY FX — it splits. Pull the lower octaves up and a

big one fills in below; pull the upper octaves up and a small one appears

above. Feed it a chord and the lower one shivers, which is the divider

telling you it has lost the note.
