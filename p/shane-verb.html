An analog reverb built around seven spaces. Pick the room first, then shape

it — decay, early reflections, character, and a full post EQ on the tail.

SPACES

Booth · Room · Chamber · Lively · Plate · Hall · Cathedral

The ghost sits inside whichever one you picked.

CONTROLS

· DECAY set the length of the tail

· PREDELAY space before the reverb starts

· MIX how much of it you hear

· BRIGHTNESS dark to bright

· CHARACTER clean to chorused

· DISTANCE close to far

· DECAY RATE scale the tail without changing the room

· STEREO WIDTH mono to wide

· DUCK reverb steps back while the source plays

· MODULATION rate and depth

· POST EQ four bands — low cut, low shelf, high shelf, high cut,

with a curve display and a reverb+post analyzer

· A/B compare and presets

WHAT'S NEW IN 1.4

· Spaces now differ clearly — Booth through Cathedral are distinct rooms, not one room resized

· Denser, smoother tail

· STEREO WIDTH now goes to 200%

· Fixed level loss when the reverb was summed to mono

INSTALL — WINDOWS

1. Unzip.

2. Copy "Shane Verb.vst3" to:

C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.

INSTALL — macOS

1. Open the .pkg installer and follow the prompts.

2. Pick the formats you want (VST3 / AU / AAX).

3. Restart your DAW and rescan plugins.

Signed and notarized by Apple — no Terminal, no security workaround.
